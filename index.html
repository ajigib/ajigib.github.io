About

I'm a doctoral researcher in Bioinformatics at the Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR), working in Dr. Jitendra Narayan's lab at CSIR-Institute of Genomics and Integrative Biology (CSIR-IGIB), New Delhi.

My thesis, Computational Discovery, Analysis and Mapping of Horizontal Gene Transfer Events in Multicellular Organisms, is at the submission stage, with the defence expected in January 2027.

Research focus

  • Comparative genomics: multi-species genome analysis, synteny reconstruction and detection of evolutionary breakpoints.
  • Horizontal gene transfer: detection using homology-based and composition-based approaches.
  • Genome annotation: ancestral genome reconstruction, repeat and transposable element annotation, functional enrichment.
  • Data processing: quality control of short-read, long-read and optical-mapping data.
  • Reproducible tools: Snakemake workflows, plus web and command-line software.
